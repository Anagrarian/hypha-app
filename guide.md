# Hypha user's guide

## Where do videos go?
| What | Where it lives | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Originals** | Your phone's gallery | Hypha never changes them. |
| **Finished videos** | Your project's album in the gallery (More → Project album shows its name) | Captions are built in. |
| **Half-finished work** (captions, parts, crop, turn, pin, front page, music) | Inside Hypha: the video list or the compost (trash), and your backups | Not a video file. Open the video again to keep working. |

- **Lost something?** Look in Hypha's list → the compost → a backup.
- **To finish a video:** open the **original**, not the finished copy (the copy would get captions twice).
- **Emptying the compost** forgets Hypha's work on those videos (a fresh start). The originals stay on your phone.
  Choosing their album again brings them back fresh.
- **Start fresh on one video:** More → Start fresh (clears its captions and edits).
- **Never in a backup:** the videos themselves (they stay in your gallery), logins, the speech engines.
