# Join the Hypha early list

Hypha adds clear captions to your videos, right on your phone. It's being built now,
one piece at a time, by a creator for creators.

## What you get
- **A peek behind the scenes:** screenshots and short notes as Hypha grows, about once a month.
- **Early versions:** try new builds before anyone else (Android for now).
- **A say in it:** short questions now and then. What you make, where you post, what's annoying.
  Your answers shape what gets built next.
- **Beta testing on Google Play**, when it opens: you'd be one of the first testers.
