# Hypha: words made clear

Hypha adds clear captions to your videos, right on your Android phone.
Your videos and words stay on your phone. No ads, no tracking.

## Install
1. On your Android phone, tap this link:
   **[Download Hypha](https://github.com/Anagrarian/hypha-app/releases/download/latest/Captions.apk)**
2. Open the downloaded file. If the phone asks, allow installing from this browser.
3. Open Hypha and follow the welcome steps (about a minute).

After that, use **Help → Update** inside Hypha to get new versions.
