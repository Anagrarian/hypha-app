# Testing Hypha

Thank you for trying Hypha! Hypha adds clear captions to your videos, right on your Android phone. You're one of the first people to use it, so you'll find rough edges. That's the point: every bump you hit makes it better for the next person.

It takes about **10 minutes**.

## 1. Install (Android only for now)
1. On your phone, tap: **[Download Hypha](https://github.com/Anagrarian/hypha-app/releases/download/latest/Captions.apk)**
2. Open the downloaded file. If the phone asks, allow installing from this browser.
   - **Samsung:** if it's blocked, go to Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker and turn it off while you install. You can turn it back on after.
3. Open Hypha.

## 2. First steps
- Follow the welcome screen (about a minute).
- Pick a folder for Hypha's work when it asks.
- Leave **daily backups** on. It's the default.

## 3. Five little tasks (about a minute each)
Use a short video of your own, 30 to 60 seconds, with someone talking.

1. **Make captions.** Add the video and let Hypha write the words.
2. **Fix a word.** Find a word it got wrong and fix it.
3. **Change the look.** Try a different caption look, size, and place on the screen.
4. **Save it.** Save the finished video, then find it in your phone's gallery.
5. **Back up.** Settings → Back up now.

If you get stuck on a task, that's useful too. Say so and move on.

## 4. Tell us how it went
The best feedback is a **screen recording while you talk out loud**: "I'm looking for... I expected... that's odd..."
- One task per recording, under a minute each.
- Send it to **hyphatheapp@proton.me** (up to 25 MB per email),
- or in Hypha: **Help → What went wrong → Send**.

Short notes are welcome too. There are no wrong answers. If something confused you, that's Hypha's fault, not yours.

## 5. If it crashes
Hypha shows a safety page after a crash. Tap **Copy**, then send it to us (paste it in an email). Then tap **Open Hypha anyway**.

Tap the round **↑** button at the top of Hypha's home screen (or Help → Update) to get the newest version.

## Your privacy
- Your videos and words stay on your phone. Hypha doesn't upload them.
- Recordings and notes you send us are used only to improve Hypha, then deleted.
