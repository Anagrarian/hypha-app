# Test Hypha on your Android phone

Hypha puts clear captions on your videos, right on your phone. No uploading, no account, no ads.
It's being built now, one piece at a time, and it needs **Android testers** before it goes on Google Play.

## Two ways in
**🌱 Try it now (download):** get Hypha straight from our GitHub page today.
**🧺 Wait for Google Play:** sign up below and we'll invite you when Play testing opens (no warnings, updates come by themselves).
You can do both: try it now *and* sign up.

## Try it now
1. On your Android phone, tap **[Download Hypha](https://github.com/Anagrarian/hypha-app/releases/download/latest/Captions.apk)** (about 130 MB).
2. Open the downloaded file. If the phone asks, allow installing from your browser.
3. Make sure there's about **400 MB free**, or the install can quietly stop.
4. **Samsung:** if it's blocked, Settings → Security and privacy → **Auto Blocker** → off while you install, then back on.
5. **"Blocked by Play Protect"?** Tap More details → Install anyway. Hypha is signed by us; Google just doesn't know it yet.
6. Then follow the 10-minute **[tester guide](testing.md)**: five little tasks and how to tell us how it went.

Updates: tap the round **↑** button on Hypha's home screen.

## What Google Play testing means (later)
- You get Hypha **free from Google Play**; an invite comes by email when testing opens.
- Keep it installed for **14 days**. Google asks for this before an app can go public.
- Tell us what's confusing or broken, if you like. A sentence is plenty.
