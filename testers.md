# Test Hypha on your Android phone

Hypha puts clear captions on your videos, right on your phone. No uploading, no account, no ads.
It's being built now, one piece at a time, and it needs **Android testers** before it goes on Google Play.

## What testing means
- You get Hypha **free from Google Play** (an invite comes by email when testing opens).
- Use it on a few of your own videos, the way you normally would.
- Keep it installed for **14 days**. Google asks for this before an app can go public.
- Tell us what's confusing or broken, if you like. A sentence is plenty.
